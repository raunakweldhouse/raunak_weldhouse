About us

company profile
Who we are
Raunak Weld House is a Delhi based supplier of TIG, MIG and plasma welding accessories, run out of New Shyam Shanti Market at Ajmeri Gate. The business is looked after by Sanjay Agarwal and Raunak Agarwal, who handle sourcing, stock and orders directly.
What we stock
Our shelves cover the parts a welding workshop reaches for most often: copper contact tips, tungsten electrodes, MIG torches, TIG torch assemblies and plasma cutting consumables. We carry MAG-WELD as our core torch and accessory line, alongside other established brands such as Wolfram Industrie and Black Wolf, so customers can match parts to the machines they already run.
Who we serve
We work with welding workshops, fabrication units and industrial buyers across Delhi NCR who need genuine, correctly specified consumables and torch parts without long wait times, whether that is a single replacement tip or a standing monthly order.

Products

current stock lines

CT-TIP

CT-Series copper contact tips

Precision copper contact tips for TIG and MIG torches, imported from Korea. Available in a range of bore sizes to match different electrode and wire diameters, so current transfer stays steady and the arc stays clean. Tip wear is predictable, which keeps torch servicing on a routine schedule rather than a surprise. Supplied in factory sealed boxes for easy stock rotation on the shelf.

WT20

Wolfram Industrie WT20 tungsten electrodes

WT20 tungsten TIG electrodes manufactured by Wolfram Industrie to EN 26848, supplied ten pieces to a box with the standard red identification band. In stock in 2.4 mm, with 3 mm and other diameters available on request. Suited to DC TIG welding of mild steel and stainless steel, giving a stable arc start and consistent electrode life through repeated re-grinds.

24KD / 36KD / 25AK

MIG welding torches, 24KD, 36KD and 25AK

MIG welding torches in the 24KD, 36KD and 25AK ranges, covering MIG, TIG and plasma cutting torch technology. Each torch comes with a moulded ergonomic handle, a flexible cable assembly and a swan neck front end, built to fit standard MIG welding machines using the matching connector. A practical spare or replacement torch for shops running daily production welding.

P80

P80 plasma cutting consumable set

P80 plasma consumable set from Black Wolf, packed ten pieces to a box and covering both the electrode and the 1.5 mm nozzle. ISO certified and quality checked before packing, these consumables are built to keep a clean, focused plasma arc through repeated cuts on standard P80 compatible plasma cutting torches. Electrodes and nozzles are stocked as matched sets so a full consumable change is always on hand.

WP-18V

TIG torch WP-18V, 4 metre

WP-18V TIG welding torch with a 4 metre hose assembly, bringing the power cable, argon gas line and coolant lines together in one flexible bundle for water cooled TIG setups. The torch head and cable are built for continuous shop use, and the extra length gives comfortable reach around larger workpieces without needing an extension. A dependable choice for longer TIG welding sessions.
